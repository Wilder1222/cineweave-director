## 代表性前后对照

1. **E09-D004**：“I'll sit. Don't lose the page.” → “I'll be okay. Just don't lose that page.” 现在是在回避海伦的背痛问题，再转回自己关心的纸。
2. **E10-D026**：“We need the document trail. Arrange legal advice before we meet, if you wish.” → “We need to know where the documents went. You can speak to a lawyer first.” 公司依旧施压，语气像面对员工当场交代。
3. **E11-D009**：“Yes. Faster isn't the same as first.” → “Yes. We'd already started.” 具体回执已承担时间因果，医生只答诺拉的问题。
4. **E12-D011**：“Regulators ask, or a court calls you, you can comply. I want that in the signed copy.” → “Regulators ask for records, or a court calls you, you can answer. I want it in writing.” 口语化没有删正式调取与作证例外。
5. **E15-D022**：“The part where I said no.” → “Use what I actually told you.” 指向旧笔记，不另送记者一句警句。
6. **E16-D013**：“I can't promise that. The disclosure is still hers. You can say no to this release.” → “I can't promise that. They can still question her about leaking records. And you can still say no.” 明确实际风险，同时保留青年拒绝权。
